Major Indian Festivals

A glimpse into the celebrations that bind millions together in joy and devotion.

Diwali

Autumn

The festival of lights symbolizing the victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

Holi

Spring

The vibrant festival of colors celebrating love, spring, and the triumph of devotion.

Navratri & Durga Puja

Winter / Autumn

Nine nights of devotion, traditional dances like Garba, and grand artistic celebrations.

Pongal & Makar Sankranti

Winter

Harvest festivals expressing gratitude to nature, the sun, and farm animals.

From the iconic Taj Mahal to the rock-cut temples of Ellora, Indian architecture showcases millennia of mathematical precision and artistic mastery.

A rich tapestry of spices, aromas, and flavors offering a unique culinary journey across every single region of the country.
